The Illinois Natural Heritage Database (INDB) is maintained by the Illinois Department of Natural Resources Division of Natural Heritage and serves as the state's official repository for records of rare, threatened, and endangered species, including birds. The database compiles verified occurrence records from field surveys, museum specimens, published literature, and agency reports, and is updated continuously as new information becomes available. For birds, the INDB includes breeding records, wintering locations, and significant migration observations for species on the Illinois Endangered and Threatened Species list. The database is used to guide environmental review for development projects, inform conservation planning, and prioritize land protection and management across the state.
