The Illinois Natural Heritage Database (INDB) is maintained by the Illinois Department of Natural Resources Division of Natural Heritage and serves as the state's official database for records of threatened and endangered species. The database compiles verified occurrence records of over 400 listed species, and is updated continuously as new information becomes available. For birds, the INDB primarily focuses on records of actively breeding threatened and endangered species, as well as wintering locations for Northern Harriers and Short-eared Owls. The database is used to guide environmental review for development projects, inform conservation planning, and prioritize land protection and management across the state.
