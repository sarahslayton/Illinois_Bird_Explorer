The Christmas Bird Count (CBC) is one of the oldest and largest citizen science programs in the world. Organized annually by the National Audubon Society since 1900, it takes place during a three-week window in late December and early January. Volunteer birders within designated 15-mile diameter count circles spend a full day tallying every bird they observe, generating a snapshot of winter bird communities across the Western Hemisphere. Illinois hosts dozens of active count circles, ranging from urban areas like Chicago and Springfield to rural bottomlands along the Illinois and Mississippi Rivers. More than a century of count data make the CBC an invaluable resource for detecting long-term changes in winter bird populations and range shifts linked to climate and land use change.
