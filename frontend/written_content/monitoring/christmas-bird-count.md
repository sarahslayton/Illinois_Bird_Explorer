The Christmas Bird Count (CBC), coordinated by the National Audubon Society, is one of the oldest and largest citizen science programs in the world. Each year, volunteer birders within designated 15-mile diameter count circles spend a full day countine every bird they observe, generating a snapshot of winter bird communities across the country. There are Illinois dozens of active count circles in Illinois, ranging from urban areas like Chicago and Springfield to rural bottomlands along the Illinois and Mississippi Rivers. More than a century of count data makes the CBC an invaluable resource for detecting long-term changes in winter bird populations.
