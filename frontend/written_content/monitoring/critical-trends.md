The Critical Trends Assessment Program (C-TAP) is a long-term ecological monitoring program operated by the Illinois Natural History Survey. Established to track the status and trajectory of biological resources across the state, C-TAP monitors breeding birds, vegetation, amphibians, and other biological indicators at systematically distributed sample sites representing Illinois's major land cover types. Bird data collected through C-TAP provide trend estimates that are independent of voluntary citizen science programs, allowing researchers to detect changes in species richness, abundance, and community composition across agricultural, urban, forested, and wetland landscapes. C-TAP data have been used to document the ongoing decline of grassland and shrubland birds in Illinois and to evaluate the ecological value of conservation lands.
