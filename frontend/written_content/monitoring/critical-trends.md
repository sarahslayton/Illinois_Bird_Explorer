The Critical Trends Assessment Program (CTAP) is a long-term ecological monitoring program coordinated by the Illinois Natural History Survey and support by the Illinois Department of Natural Resources. Since 1997, scientists have collected data on breeding birds, plants, and arthropods at over 600 randomly selected sites across grasslands, woodlands, streams, and wetlands  in Illinois. By combining data about birds with plant and insect data at a particular site, these surveys can reveal changes in the environment that may be impacting avian populations across different habitat types, and over time.
