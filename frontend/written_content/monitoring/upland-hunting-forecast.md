Each year, the Illinois Department of Natural Resources releases an upland hunting forecast based on summer population surveys conducted earlier in the season. The forecast provides hunters, land managers, and the public with expectations for Ring-necked Pheasant, Northern Bobwhite, Wild Turkey, and other upland species heading into the fall and winter hunting seasons. It reflects habitat conditions, weather patterns during the breeding season, and population indices measured through standardized survey routes. The forecast is an important communication tool that connects monitoring data to practical hunting management decisions and highlights areas of concern for species showing population declines.
