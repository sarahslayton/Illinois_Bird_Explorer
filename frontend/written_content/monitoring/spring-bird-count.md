The Illinois Spring Bird Count is conducted annually on the first Saturday of May, organized by the Illinois Ornithological Society. Volunteer birders across all 102 Illinois counties systematically observe and record every bird species encountered throughout the day, generating one of the largest coordinated single-day bird inventories of any state in the country. Because early May coincides with the peak of spring migration through Illinois, the count captures a broad cross-section of both resident breeding species and migrants passing through on their way to northern breeding grounds. Decades of count records provide trend data on the timing and composition of spring migration and offer an annual measure of the state's avifaunal diversity.
