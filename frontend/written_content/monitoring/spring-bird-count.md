The Illinois Spring Bird Count is one of the longest-running statewide bird counts in North America. On a single day each May, volunteer birders across all 102 Illinois counties systematically observe and record every bird species they encounter, generating one of the largest coordinated single-day bird inventories of any state in the country. Because early May coincides with the peak of spring migration through Illinois, the count captures a broad cross-section of both resident breeding species and migrants passing through on their way to northern breeding grounds.
