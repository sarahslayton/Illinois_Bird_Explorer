For hundreds of species of migratory birds, Illinois serves as critical stopover habitat between their breeding and wintering grounds. From the shoreline of Lake Michigan to the forested swamps in the far south, the state offers a mosaic of habitats that support millions of birds each spring and fall. Identifying and protecting these stopover sites is essential, as migrating birds depend on these habitats to rest and refuel after long overnight flights. Migration is the most dangerous period of a bird's life, and loss of key stopover sites can have cascading effects on populations across entire flyways.
