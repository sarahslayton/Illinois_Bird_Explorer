Illinois lies within the heart of the Mississippi Flyway — one of North America's four major migratory corridors — making it a critical stopover for hundreds of species traveling between breeding grounds in the north and wintering areas to the south. From the shoreline of Lake Michigan to the forested river bottoms of the far south, the state offers a mosaic of habitats that support millions of birds each spring and fall. Identifying and protecting these stopover sites is essential: birds arriving after long overnight flights depend on finding food and shelter quickly, and loss of key stopover habitat can have cascading effects on populations across entire flyways.
