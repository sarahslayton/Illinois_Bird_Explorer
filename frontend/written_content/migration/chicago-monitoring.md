The Chicago Bird Migration Monitoring Network is a collaborative research effort to systematically monitor bird migration across Chicagoland. They use radar, thermal imagery, bioacoustic monitoring, and other methods to advance the study of bird migration and gain valuable information about how hundreds of species migrate througha highly urban landscape. Chicago's position on the southwestern shore of Lake Michigan creates a natural concentration point for migrants moving along the shoreline, making it one of the most significant urban migration monitoring areas in North America. This program also aims to raise awareness about migration and conservation efforts throughout the region.
