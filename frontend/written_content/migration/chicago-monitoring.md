The Chicago Bird Migration Monitoring Network coordinates systematic monitoring of bird migration across the Chicago metropolitan region. Using standardized count protocols at consistent locations over multiple seasons, the network generates long-term data on which species are moving through, in what numbers, and when — information valuable for understanding population trends, habitat use, and the effects of urbanization on migratory birds. Chicago's position on the southwestern shore of Lake Michigan creates a natural concentration point for migrants moving along the shoreline, making it one of the most significant urban migration monitoring areas in North America. Data from the network complement broader citizen-science platforms and contribute to regional and continental assessments of bird population health.
