The Forbes Biological Station, operated by the Illinois Natural History Survey, has conducted systematic aerial surveys of waterfowl across Illinois for decades. These inventories provide one of the longest continuous records of waterfowl abundance and distribution in the Midwest, documenting the numbers and locations of ducks, geese, and other waterbirds as they migrate through and winter in Illinois. Survey data are used by wildlife managers to monitor population trends, assess habitat conditions along the Mississippi and Illinois River corridors, and inform hunting regulations and wetland conservation priorities at the state and regional level.
