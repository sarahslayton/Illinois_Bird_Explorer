The Illinois Natural History Survey’s Forbes Biological Station conducts annual surveys of waterfowl populations across 41 locations in the Mississippi and Illinois river valleys. Aerial inventories are conducted by a pilot and highly skilled biologist flying in a fixed wing aircraft, who counts the number of birds present as the plane carefully flies over each survey area. These surveys are intended to document the distribution of waterfowl species throughout these monitored regions and provide a consistent metric to assess temporal changes in waterfowl populations. The data from these aerial inventories is used to direct waterfowl management, research, land acquisition decisions, and for public outreach efforts.
