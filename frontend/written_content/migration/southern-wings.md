Southern Wings is a collaborative, partner-led program focused on the full annual cycle conservation of migratory birds that spend at least eight months outside of the United States. This program is coordinated by the Association of Fish and Wildlife Agencies, and funds on-the-ground habitat conservation by matching contributions from U.S. state wildlife agencies with partner organizations operating in wintering areas. This group facilitates agency’s participation in research, conservation projects, and management efforts in Central and South America, Mexico, and the Caribbean.
