Southern Wings is a cooperative program that supports the conservation of migratory birds on their wintering grounds in Latin America and the Caribbean. Administered through the Association of Fish and Wildlife Agencies, the program funds on-the-ground habitat conservation by matching contributions from U.S. state wildlife agencies with partner organizations operating in wintering areas. Because many birds that breed or pass through Illinois spend five or more months in Central America, South America, or the Caribbean, protecting habitat in those regions is as critical to population health as protecting breeding and stopover habitat here. Southern Wings makes it possible for state wildlife agencies to invest directly in international conservation through a structured, accountable framework.
