The Midwest Migration Network is a regional collaboration of researchers, bird observatories, and conservation agencies working to coordinate migration monitoring across the interior United States. By standardizing methods and pooling data across state and organizational boundaries, the network generates a broader, more consistent picture of how bird populations are moving through the continental interior — information that is essential for understanding migration ecology, detecting population changes, and directing conservation resources to where they are needed most. Illinois's central location within the flyway makes it a key contributor to this regional picture.
